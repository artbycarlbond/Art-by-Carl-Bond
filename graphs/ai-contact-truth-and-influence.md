## Pressure

Use this graph when the pressure is:

- fluent AI output that wants trust
- a public claim,
  image,
  plan,
  story,
  metric,
  dashboard,
  policy,
  or statement asking to be believed
- a claim that powerful actors coordinated public harm,
  or public attention turning toward a named target

  or word choice changing access,
  ranking,

## Claim Discipline

Sort claims into:

- verified
- alleged
- disputed
- inferred
- unknown
- false
- outdated
- context-dependent

Then ask:

- What exact sentence needs checking?
- What source trail supports it?
- What contradicts it?
- Who benefits if it is believed quickly?
- What timeline matters?
- What harm,
  duty,
  money,
  rights,
  privacy,
  safety,
  or public consequence follows?
- What is the smallest verified version?
- What would correction require?

## Stops

Stop when:

- the claim has a status
- the missing evidence is named
- the source trail is external to the model
- the confidence has been scaled to evidence
- the next action belongs to a person,
  record,
  source,
  or qualified reviewer
- `stewardship/operating-discipline.md` says the model must not decide

## Do Not Turn This Into

- a debate manual
- a canon spine
- a public accusation machine
- a current-events feed
- cynicism dressed as rigor
- trust in AI because it sounds careful
- distrust of every institution because institutions can drift
