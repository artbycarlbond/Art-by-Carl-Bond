## What This Map Is

This map shows how the Ring 2 source shelf is organized.

Ring 1 is the workbench and Ring 2 is the source shelf.

Ring 1 helps you act.
Ring 2 shows the pressure behind the method.

Source shelf files are not active routes,
not current doctrine,
and not required reading.

They preserve pressure,
witness,
and selected perception language.

Active practice remains in Ring 1.

## Boundary

```text
source-shelf/README.md
  -> active root archive
```

This boundary prevents source shelf material from becoming the public entrance.

Older working source may exist outside this two-ring taxonomy for audit and migration review.
It is not a ring,
not a public route,
and not a deeper canon.

## Transfer Rule

Do not move source pressure because it is interesting.

Move only source pressure that supports a reader job the archive already needs:

- art creation and local return
- AI contact and truth
- commons,
  access,
  memory,
  and maintenance
- work,
  tools,
  money,
  and capacity
- boundary,
  refusal,
  repair,
  and growth discipline

## Stop Rule

Stop when the topic has one of these statuses:

- active:
  Ring 1 already carries it
- covered:
  Ring 2 preserves pressure and Ring 1 has a route
- candidate:
  `stewardship/concept-register.md` should hold it
- do not import:
  it would create bloat,
  current-claim churn,
  personal mythology,
  or a second archive
