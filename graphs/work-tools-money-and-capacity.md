## Capacity Test

Ask:

- What does this strengthen?
- What does this weaken?
- What cost moved?
- Who pays,
  profits,
  and carries risk?
- What record remains?
- Who can inspect,
  correct,
  appeal,
  refuse,
  export,
  repair,
  or leave?
- What judgment remains human?
- What capacity remains after use?

## Stops

Stop when:

- the tool's owner,
  cost,
  record,
  exit,
  and repair path are named
- the money claim has returned to lived use
- worker standing and contestability are clear
- the cost needs outside verification
- legal,
  tax,
  financial,
  employment,
  benefits,
  or high-stakes claims require qualified review
