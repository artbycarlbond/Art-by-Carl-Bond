## Route Rule

Choose one file.
Carry one standard.
Stop at one next action,
verification need,
refusal,
repair,
or handoff to a person.

If two files are needed,
name why before opening the second.

## Pressure Routes

### Inquiry Pressure

Use [methods/inquiry.md](methods/inquiry.md) when you need the next better question,
not the whole answer.

Standard:
ask clearly before accepting fluency.

Stop when:
you can name the next question.

### Claim Pressure

Use [methods/truth-and-claims.md](methods/truth-and-claims.md) when something wants belief,
circulation,
money,
policy,
trust,
or action.

Standard:
separate claim,
evidence,
inference,
uncertainty,
risk,
and next action.

Stop when:
you know what can be carried forward
and what still needs evidence.

### Contact Pressure

Use [methods/ai-contact.md](methods/ai-contact.md) when an abstraction starts to feel complete:
a model answer,
a price,
a screen balance,
a dashboard,
a ranking,
or a policy label.

Standard:
abstraction must return to Body,
Record,
Consequence,
Commons,
and Capacity.

Stop when:
the next contact with reality is clear.

### Boundary Pressure

Use [stewardship/operating-discipline.md](stewardship/operating-discipline.md) when the work can affect real people,
money,
privacy,
safety,
rights,
health,
housing,
employment,
or external systems.

Standard:
the model may help;
the human keeps authority,
responsibility,
and consequence.

Stop when:
the boundary,
owner,
permission,
and repair path are clear.
