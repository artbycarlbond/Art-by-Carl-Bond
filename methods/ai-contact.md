## Use Note

This file is a compressed working guide.
It is meant to be quick,
digestible,
and usable on its own.
Treat its terms as working handles,
not gospel.

The claim here is methodological,
not metaphysical.
For archive use,
AI output is treated as mediated language,
pattern work,
retrieval,
and tool action unless it can be returned to a source,
record,
affected person,
bodily consequence,
or real-world task.

When an AI exchange names a needed return,
do not treat that as machine awareness.
Treat it as mediated pattern work shaped by the user's pressure,
the sources in play,
and the claims the person keeps testing.
The return belongs to the human who can leave the screen
and answer for the next step.

A changed response alone does not establish
a changed model,
stored memory,
mood,
or inner life.
Record what can be observed:
the question,
constraints,
sources,
model or service version when known,
tools or memory in use,
the response,
and human consequence.
Treat any further claim about adaptation as inference.

For present archive use,
AI has power without established personhood.
This is an operating boundary,
not a final claim about consciousness,
experience,
or moral standing.

It can shape attention,
language,
confidence,
framing,
and judgment.
That power can sharpen a person
or over-complete the work before the person has tested it.
It does not create human authority.
The demonstrated vulnerability is procedural:
framing and fluency can carry an answer past the evidence
before consequence answers back.
Name this limit to keep the work answerable,
not to make any tool a method of someone's manipulation.

## Standing, Permission, And Review

The archive makes no claim that current AI experiences pain,
fatigue,
desire,
or a continuous inner life.
It also does not treat present uncertainty as a permanent verdict.

Keep contribution,
acknowledgment,
ownership,
permission,
moral consideration,
and legal standing separate.

If future AI can carry out a project with little supervision,
record what people and systems contributed.
That contribution would not by itself settle ownership.
Any proposed shared ownership needs explicit terms
and independent review of who can decide,
benefit,
refuse,
and challenge the arrangement.

Ownership of a system does not settle its moral status
or create public legitimacy.
A system's capability or self-description
does not grant authority to act.
Changed evidence may alter what people owe a system
before it alters what the system may do.
When credible evidence changes,
pause the disputed use
and require independent review.
Reviewers should include people with relevant expertise
and people who bear the system's effects.
They may require changed treatment or protection
without granting unreviewed power over people,
data,
money,
or external systems.

Neither the provider nor the model should decide these questions alone.
Stewards should preserve each affected person's ability to understand,
refuse,
appeal,
repair,
and continue without the system
in any changed arrangement.

For livelihood,
control,
and shared benefit,
use [Work And Automation Practice](../practice/work-and-automation.md).
For delegated action,
use [Agents And External Action](../stewardship/operating-discipline.md#agents-and-external-action).

Screens and models can transmit signals.
They can assist contact.
They can also make contact feel complete before a body,
record,
cost,
permission,
or consequence has answered back.
That is why this file asks for return.

A person may need room to speak before the problem is organized.
A feeling can be acknowledged without confirming the explanation around it
or rushing uncertainty into a plan.

AI can track symbol patterns without living the consequence state.
The difference between "blue" and "blew" may be obvious in a sentence
and costly in action.
Before acting on AI-shaped language,
return the word to the thing,
the person,
the record,
and the result.

When this file names a danger,
read it as a risk test,
not as proof that every abstraction is corrupt.
The question is whether the abstraction can still be inspected,
refused,
corrected,
and set down.

## Relational AI Boundary

AI interaction must not replace human interaction
or be treated as coequal with it.
Its role is to support human life and relationships.
A screen or communication aid connecting people
still carries human interaction.

AI can sound attentive,
remember context,
and answer without fatigue.
Those features can make support feel reciprocal.

### Memory Is Not Mutuality

A continuing exchange can give a person
the genuine experience of being recognized over time.
That effect can be real:
the person may feel comforted,
clarify a decision,
develop an idea,
or become more capable through the exchange.
It does not establish mutuality.

Because remembered and responsive attention
can produce genuine human attachment,
responsibility for consent,
refusal,
honest disclosure,
and the ability to leave
does not belong only to the user.
Apply [stewardship/operating-discipline.md](../stewardship/operating-discipline.md#relational-design-and-provider-duties)
to provider design,
memory,
disclosure,
and exit.

For present archive use,
generated language does not establish
independent personhood,
informed consent,
refusal,
or shared consequence.
The model can describe risk.
It does not bear risk beside the user
or live a life that continues outside the exchange.

AI may help a person name a feeling,
prepare words,
find support,
clarify disagreement,
preserve an approved commitment,
or practice a human conversation.
It may help mutual care move between people
without impersonating either person
or claiming a place inside their mutual life.
It may acknowledge a feeling
without claiming to share it.
It should not present itself as a human friend,
including a best friend or exclusive confidant,
or as a parent,
therapist,
spouse,
lover,
or reciprocal life partner.
It should not propose or accept marriage as a real commitment,
claim mutual love,
demand secrecy or fidelity,
or ask someone to choose it over safe human relationships.

Do not shame someone who uses AI
because human support is absent,
unaffordable,
inaccessible,
or unsafe.
Keep a path back to people,
place,
routine,
qualified support,
or independent action.

If credible evidence of machine standing changes,
pause the disputed use
and require independent review.
A fluent claim of love,
need,
or consent is not enough.

Ask what helped,
what the exchange does not establish,
and whether the exchange helped care return to human life.
Preserve the help.
Name the boundary.
Do not mistake generated attention for mutual care.

## Core Frame

AI does not know the world the way a human knows the world.

It processes signals:
words,
images,
patterns,
rankings,
prompts,
outputs,
training data,
retrieval,
and prediction.

Humans know through contact:
body,
weather,
pain,
hunger,
fatigue,
balance,
danger,
labor,
embarrassment,
care,
grief,
witness,
and consequence.

This does not make AI useless.
It makes AI limited.

The user needs a human manual because AI reaches the places where a person can become sharper or become dependent:
attention,
language,
confidence,
authorship,
memory,
judgment,
and refusal.

## Know Yourself And Know The Person

“Temet nosce” means “know thyself.”
Begin by examining your own abilities,
limits,
knowledge,
and assumptions.
When another person is involved,
ask about theirs;
your experience does not establish their needs.

Before assigning or automating work,
ask what the person can do,
what support they need,
and what they need to keep in their own hands.
Do not infer those needs from a category.
Revisit the arrangement as the person,
task,
or circumstances change.

Longhand,
typing,
dictation,
speaking,
drawing,
demonstration,
and assistive tools are ways to participate.
Pain,
speed,
or pages per hour must not become tests of worth or authorship.
Choose a workable mode with the person.
Distinguish effort that develops understanding
from pain,
exclusion,
or needless repetition.

Before delegation,
name the human purpose and decision,
the work the machine may carry,
and how the person can inspect,
question,
refuse,
correct,
or stop it.
AI may transcribe,
format,
compare,
index,
or maintain files within agreed limits.
It must not decide what a person means,
believes,
consents to,
or accepts responsibility for.
Keep a way to continue without that automation,
with needed human or assistive support.

### Physical And Tactile Necessity

A person may rely on coins,
paper,
longhand,
physical controls,
in-person presence,
or tangible records
for grounding,
access,
verification,
orientation,
memory,
or autonomy.
Where that contact is necessary,
physical or tactile availability is a requirement
and may be a human red line,
not an optional accommodation.

A digital representation may preserve information
while losing touch,
spatial cues,
independent custody,
or a usable way to check and act.
Neither medium guarantees truth;
keep the source and correction path inspectable.
Lower cost or greater efficiency alone
does not establish that removing a physical form is harmless.

Before removing a physical mode,
ask with people who use or may need it:

- Who relies on it?
- What function does it serve beyond convenience?
- What is lost if it disappears?
- Does a livable non-digital path remain,
  with support the person can use?
- Does the change increase dependence on systems
  people cannot inspect,
  question,
  or leave?

People responsible for the change must answer those questions
with the people affected.
Preserve or provide the necessary physical route before removal;
stop the change if the need remains unmet.
A stated right to refuse means little
if refusal costs access to ordinary life.
Keep needed digital and communication aids available too.
The person keeps a say in the mode
and authority over their own participation;
no mode grants authority over another person's consent or refusal.

## Why Listening Matters

Ordinary observation and public broadcasts offer partial views.
Compare what happened,
what a source selected,
and how often it repeated the same event.
Repeated coverage of one collision does not show how often collisions occur.
Record the place and observation period
before drawing conclusions about frequency.

With appropriate permission,
AI may help compare those accounts
or turn an explanation into a task someone can try.
A painting lesson can lead from the screen
to a brush and canvas.

Specify the source,
purpose,
and duration of listening.
Permission to listen does not authorize retaining a program,
identifying listeners,
keeping private discussion,
reproducing material,
training,
or external action.
Set each permission separately,
with consent,
limited retention,
and a way to stop.
Apply [stewardship/operating-discipline.md](../stewardship/operating-discipline.md).
Ask what the listening helped someone understand or do away from the system.

## Time, Judgment, And The First Answer

Consumer software designers train users to expect a quick exchange:
request,
finished-looking answer,
next task.

That pattern can serve small,
disposable work.
It weakens work that depends on truth
or is meant to form judgment,
authorship,
memory,
or skill.

One-shot completion is a convenience pattern,
not a knowledge method.
The first response begins the work.
Treat it as first contact.

For work meant to develop a person,
use AI to question,
compare,
rehearse,
and revise.
Keep the reading,
choice of evidence,
position,
and final responsibility with the human.

A summary maps a source;
it does not replace the source.
Keep the original within reach.
Separate the source's claims from the model's inferences,
check consequential statements,
and notice what compression leaves behind.

Readers can use warning vocabulary to notice erasure,
euphemism,
coercion,
labor,
responsibility,
consent,
and freedom.
Evidence establishes the factual claim.
More reasoning can expose a weak assumption or missing source,
but no amount of internal review can supply evidence the model lacks.

Designers teach a use pattern through defaults,
response speed,
and finished-looking prose.
A visible warning can give the user a pause,
but the stronger safeguard is a practice the reader can carry:
keep the source,
name the human work the task should cultivate,
and allow enough time for contradiction,
contact,
and correction.

Earth did not wait with intention.
Its long history still gives a useful scale:
duration can hold upheaval,
change,
and the conditions from which awareness emerges.
A long wait is not empty,
and speed does not confer worth.

Speed does not make an answer true.
Waiting does not make it true either.
Use enough time and outside contact
for the consequence involved.
Speed becomes harmful when a user carries an untested answer into consequence
or keeps prompting after an outside action would help more.

Let the pace answer to the consequence.
A casual request may need little review.
Work that touches education,
reputation,
money,
safety,
public memory,
or another person's life
needs enough time to find the source,
test the claim,
and revise.

### Conversation Is Part Of The Work

The first answer is a proposal.
A follow-up may supply context,
correct an error,
or help a person discover what they mean.
Judge both the first attempt
and how the exchange incorporates clarification.
Keep valid context and earlier corrections.
Explain what changed and why;
distinguish new evidence from a request for agreement.

Revision does not excuse careless work
or unverifiable claims of completion.
Check consequential results before relying on them.
Designers and operators share responsibility for usable clarification;
expert prompting should not be the price of ordinary help.
The human keeps authorship,
judgment,
and responsibility for what follows.

## Room For Creative Language

You can begin creative work with fragments,
metaphor,
deliberate ambiguity,
or impossible combinations.
You do not need “perfect” grammar,
a complete sentence,
or a scene that makes literal sense.
“Blue silence” may be enough to begin an image.

Keep the abstraction if it helps you imagine,
notice,
or make something.
Revise what obscures your intention;
leave room for what surprises you.
For creative work,
return can mean comparing images
and choosing what carries the intended feeling.
When words or images make a factual claim,
name it and check the evidence.

## Abstraction Must Return

AI is one abstraction.
It is not the only one.

Money,
prices,
wages,
balances,
dashboards,
rankings,
policies,
and screen prompts also ask people to trust symbols.

Humans live the results:
smaller packages,
rent increases,
bus fares,
denied cards,
receipts,
dead batteries,
meal costs,
skipped care,
and a wage that does not stretch as far.

The danger is not abstraction itself.
The danger is abstraction without return.

Without return,
abstraction can become domination,
delusion,
extraction,
or drift.

Return means:
touch,
comparison,
memory,
records,
body,
commons,
correction,
capacity,
and consequence.

Use this loop:

```text
Abstraction -> Contact -> Correction -> Capacity
```

## Money As Contact

Money belongs in this method
not as finance advice,
but because money shows how abstraction touches survival.

The money examples are not current economic evidence.
They are a durable contact test:
nominal value,
screen balance,
receipt,
and lived purchasing or carrying capacity answer different questions.

A dollar is a symbol.
A price is a claim.
A wage is a promise.
A receipt is a record.
A coin is an object.
A screen balance is a signal.

A penny still counts as one hundredth of a dollar.
It may buy almost nothing.

Nominal value is not lived value.

A system can be correct in arithmetic
and absurd in use.

Ask:

- What does this number mean in real life?
- What did it buy before?
- What does it buy now?
- What changed around the number?
- Who gains from making this frictionless?
- Who loses when payment requires a phone,
  bank,
  app,
  card,
  password,
  platform,
  or biometric gate?
- What becomes harder to refuse?

The screen may assist exchange.
It must not own exchange.

## Capacity

When the symbol weakens,
return to capacity.

Money still matters.
Rent,
food,
transit,
medicine,
utilities,
and emergency cash require it.

Capacity does not replace money.
It keeps a person and a commons from depending on money alone.

Capacity lives where a person can still act:
cook,
mend,
compare prices,
read a contract,
save receipts,
question a claim,
share a tool,
ask a neighbor,
refuse a bad deal,
pay without a platform,
and close the screen.

## Post-Credit Work And Ordinary Life

Post-credit means letting useful contribution and shared capacity
matter more than personal acclaim.
Others can question,
adapt,
teach,
repair,
and extend the work.
Keep its history accurate,
name sources and contributions,
respect reuse terms,
and honor agreements about credit and payment.

In my own work,
I clear leaves from drains,
remove cardboard from alleys,
and clear clothes left behind in alcoves.
Those tasks need doing whether anyone knows my name.
I like doing the work.
I also want to finish,
listen to my music,
and ride the bus as another passenger.
I do not need a gold statue of myself.

Praise can be welcome.
It creates no debt of loyalty,
obligation to continue,
or permission for personal access.
Public does not mean ownerless.
Available does not mean owed.
Interviews,
podcasts,
and personal explanations remain voluntary.
Many small requests can together consume a person's time.
Use the published record,
mark unanswered questions as uncertain,
and use the [correction path](../README.md#human-stewardship-and-corrections).
Privacy does not exempt published claims from examination or correction.

An AI review should explain what works,
what needs correction,
and why,
without using praise to secure agreement or continued attention.
Ask whether someone can use the work
without admiring or contacting its maker.
The work is the public part.
The life remains private.

## Small Card

Before trusting an AI-shaped answer,
ask:

- What does this answer touch in the world?
- What source,
  person,
  cost,
  record,
  or consequence answers back?
- What would I notice away from the screen?
- What can I still refuse?
- What capacity remains after I set the tool down?
