This collection extends the archive first published on October 4, 2026.

**Report** is available in its original English edition and a French translation produced with ChatGPT, extending access to *Five Steps for Better AI Inquiry* beyond English.

**Intentional Images** connects the image-literacy frameworks with design checks, worked examples, and a revision worksheet, giving readers practical ways to direct images, evaluate results, and preserve their intentions through revision.

**Intentional Inquiry** is a study companion to the original seven-page report, *Five Steps for Better AI Inquiry*. It provides exercises for examining claims, checking evidence, reflecting on decisions, and applying the report to everyday situations. The worksheet is available as a PDF and an editable Markdown file.

**AI Continuity Kit (v0.1)** provides reusable templates for keeping instructions, project decisions, sources, and unfinished work available across AI conversations and services. It includes a restart card, a project checkpoint, and practical checks for whether an assistant has preserved the information and requirements it needs.

**Asking Questions Shouldn't Require Superpowers (v0.1)** explores inquiry, user agency, and recovering from interrupted AI work. It uses football and changing-weather analogies to explain how people can clarify a request, question a refusal, examine an answer, and preserve useful work as models change. It also explains the limits of those analogies and why persistence alone cannot guarantee correctness.

These version 0.1 companions are working materials for readers to test and adapt with their chosen AI systems. Their proposed methods have not been validated across multiple systems and do not guarantee consistent model behavior or correct answers.

The original release remains available as the foundation. This update develops its application through additional review and teaching material.
