### Human Ground Condition

This archive does not assume that human life must be justified through labor.

Existence is not a performance contract.
Survival is not a reward system.
Dignity is not transactional.

No person should be forced into permanent competition for:
- food
- shelter
- healthcare
- rest
- safety
- continuity

A system that requires constant struggle to remain alive is not a civilization.

This archive is built on a different civil assumption:

Human worth is not conditional.

The purpose of systems is not to extract value from people.
The purpose of systems is to protect life.
