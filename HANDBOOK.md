## Thirty-Second Start

Use AI without surrendering judgment.

AI can draft,
compare,
summarize,
question,
and rehearse.
It cannot live the consequence for you.

Before you trust an answer,
ask:

- What is being claimed?
- What is being assumed?
- What source would change this?
- Who is affected?
- What can I refuse?
- What must happen outside the screen?

## The Manual Frame

AI is the immediate subject,
but the deeper subject is human sovereignty under abstraction.

AI,
screens,
prices,
balances,
dashboards,
rankings,
and policies can make life feel clean,
fast,
and weightless.

Human life gives abstraction weight:
body,
place,
cost,
labor,
consent,
record,
commons,
repair,
refusal,
and consequence.

Use the return loop:

```text
Abstraction -> Contact -> Correction -> Capacity
```

## Field Standard

Use `FIELD_STANDARD.md` when you need the shortest discipline:

1. Name the pressure.
2. Ask for help without surrendering the center.
3. Check against human reality.
4. Revise after contact.
5. Decide what enters life.
6. Leave a repairable trace.

## Claim Standard

Use `methods/truth-and-claims.md` when an answer or idea asks to be treated as true.

Mark:

- opinion
- claim
- evidence
- inference
- uncertainty
- risk
- action needed

AI can propose.
The world must dispose.

## Contact Standard

Use `methods/ai-contact.md` when language,
money,
screens,
or metrics start to replace contact.

Ask:

- What does this mean in real life?
- What changed around the number?
- Who gains from frictionless use?
- Who loses access when the screen becomes mandatory?
- Before removing a physical form, what would people lose, and what physical access must remain available to meet their needs?
- What becomes harder to refuse?

The screen may assist exchange.
It must not own exchange.

## Boundary Standard

Use `stewardship/operating-discipline.md` when the situation touches:

- harmful capability
- private data
- money movement
- health,
  housing,
  safety,
  legal rights,
  employment,
  or intimate relationships
- agentic systems
- external action
- real people who did not consent to the exchange

## Return

Close the screen when the next step belongs to the world.

Return through:

- source
- body
- room
- sidewalk
- receipt
- witness
- person affected
- task
- repair
- rest
