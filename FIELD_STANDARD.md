## The Standard

AI is a tool with concrete consequences.
It should remain an instrument,
not an oracle,
leash,
therapist,
judge,
or final authority.

AI may help a person prepare for human contact.
AI interaction must not replace human interaction
or be treated as coequal with it.
Keep the model in a supporting role.
For present archive use,
generated responses do not supply
the independent consent,
refusal,
duty,
or shared consequence
required for reciprocal partnership or marriage.

Any tool that touches judgment,
memory,
authorship,
confidence,
or refusal must answer to human sovereignty.

Human worth does not come from employment,
productivity,
wealth,
market demand,
or a machine's need for labor.

The field question is:

> Does this exchange leave the human more able to verify,
> refuse,
> repair,
> decide,
> act,
> and set the tool down?

Apply that question to the archive too.
Invite scrutiny from people with different experience
and relevant knowledge.
Ask what the archive overlooks,
whom its advice fits poorly,
what benefits it undervalues,
and what evidence could change its conclusions.
Use that review to correct,
remove,
or add what improves a reader's understanding or choices.
Keep the archive compact
and this review practice open to revision.

The operating loop is:

```text
Abstraction -> Contact -> Correction -> Capacity
```

Abstraction can help.
Do not let it rule without return.

## The Five Returns

Return means checking abstraction against five forms of lived reality:

1. **Body**:
   What does this ask of a living person:
   attention,
   fatigue,
   time,
   hunger,
   fear,
   movement,
   rest,
   or care?

2. **Record**:
   What source,
   receipt,
   log,
   timeline,
   quote,
   observation,
   or preserved uncertainty can answer back?

3. **Consequence**:
   Who changes after this is believed,
   bought,
   shared,
   automated,
   ranked,
   refused,
   or acted on?

4. **Commons**:
   Who else can question,
   use,
   refuse,
   improve,
   contest,
   or repair this?
   In public-power cases,
   name who has standing,
   access,
   and a repair path.

5. **Capacity**:
   Is the person stronger after setting the tool down:
   more able to verify,
   refuse,
   repair,
   decide,
   act,
   rest,
   or return to the world?

A return does not have to be large.
One source,
one walk,
one receipt,
one affected person,
one refusal condition,
or one next task can be enough.

## The Loop

1. Name the claim and pressure.
   What is being asserted?
   Is it known,
   unknown,
   inferred,
   opinion,
   suspicion,
   prediction,
   metaphor,
   or feeling?
   What is being decided,
   believed,
   built,
   bought,
   shared,
   automated,
   or carried into another person's life?

2. Ask for help without surrendering the center.
   Let AI compare,
   draft,
   summarize,
   question,
   rehearse,
   or widen the frame.

3. Check against human reality.
   What evidence is present?
   What source,
   body,
   room,
   person,
   cost,
   consent,
   record,
   or consequence must answer back?
   Who benefits if this enters life before contact?
   Who carries cost if it fails?

4. Revise after contact.
   If the answer fails against the world,
   narrow it,
   correct it,
   refuse it,
   or stop.

5. Decide what enters life.
   The model may assist.
   The human keeps judgment,
   consent,
   risk,
   authorship,
   and consequence.

6. Leave a repairable trace.
   Keep the source,
   note uncertainty,
   name the next action,
   preserve the record,
   or return to the person,
   task,
   place,
   body,
   or rest.

When a conclusion will be shared or used again,
keep enough for another reader to examine it
without reconstructing the conversation.
Keep the source or observation within reach,
including evidence that challenges the conclusion.
Say what each person or AI actually checked,
what remains uncertain,
and what could change the answer.
Keep the reason for a correction attached.
An earlier answer is not a fresh check.
Different readers may reach agreement,
disagree,
or leave the question open.
Keep enough detail for independent checking;
a short record can be enough.

## Human Speed Checkpoint

AI may generate at machine speed.
Human judgment should return at human speed before it is trusted.

Walk with the answer if you can.
If walking is not available,
return through body,
place,
source,
person,
record,
task,
or rest.

A long exchange may need a longer return:
an errand,
a meal,
a repaired object,
a cleaned room,
a paper note,
a visit,
or a conversation with someone affected.

A return can also be strenuous,
observational,
or quiet:
a hard run,
weather felt without capture,
a sunset,
or traffic watched from enough distance to recover scale.

If an answer only works inside the chat,
it is not finished.

## Optional Lens: Material Questions

The Five Returns are the core method.
Use this optional lens only when it clarifies what kind of question you are holding.

- **Clay**:
  what is still forming
  and should not harden yet?
- **Glass**:
  what is clear,
  reflected,
  distorted,
  or fragile?
- **Steel**:
  what must carry weight,
  scale,
  rule,
  risk,
  repair,
  or record?
- **Diamond**:
  what survives pressure,
  correction,
  ego,
  fear,
  and fluency?

Objects are applied tests,
not foundations.

- A key is metal shaped into access.
- A window is glass shaped into visibility.
- A ring is metal shaped into bond.
- Rebar is steel shaped into hidden support.
- Paper is fiber shaped into record.
- Rope is fiber shaped into connection and tension.

Materials ask what kind of pressure the question can survive.
Objects ask what human use the question has been shaped to serve.

## Stop Conditions

Stop or slow down when:

- the answer feels smooth but cannot be checked
- the task involves health,
  safety,
  housing,
  money,
  employment,
  legal rights,
  or intimate relationships
- private data,
  credentials,
  records,
  or third-party information are exposed
- a public claim could direct anger,
  threats,
  harassment,
  release of private details,
  or punishment by association
  toward a person or group
  before the source trail is clear
- an agent,
  tool,
  automation,
  or system may act outside the chat
- the exchange makes refusal,
  verification,
  repair,
  or leaving harder
- the chat keeps absorbing attention without returning capacity

Speed is not sovereignty.
Return is the checkpoint.
